Deployment sleep strategies to cope with mental health and wellbeing

Tuesday, June 17, 2025, 2:00 PM PST
Featured Mental Well-Being
Description: Are you sailing the high seas and concerned about sleeping well? Do you want to learn sleep fundamentals to enhance your operational readiness? Healthy sleep powers the mind, restores the body, and is essential for cognitive processes. This webinar explores important tips for getting great sleep at sea — how to stay comfortable, get to sleep in a sea environment, and develop a sleep routine. (45 minutes)
